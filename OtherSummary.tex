\section{Other Constructions}



Beyond the core Waterfall construction, we develop two complementary generic
DMPF variants for specific parameter regimes.  The Ring-LPN-specialized
Reverse-Cuckoo optimization is presented separately in
\sectionref{sec:revCuckoo}.

\paragraph{Bucketing.}
When indices are (approximately) uniform, we partition the domain into $k$ buckets and apply a
DMPF independently per bucket on a smaller subdomain. With $k \approx t/\log t$, standard
concentration bounds imply that each bucket contains $O(\log t)$ items with high probability,
yielding an overall $O(t)$-scale key-generation and evaluation profile (up to logarithmic factors),
and allowing substantial parallelism. The group-by (frequency counting and padded regrouping)
machinery and parameterization details are provided in the appendix. See \sectionref{sec:bucketing} for details.

\paragraph{Big-State.}
We also cover the big-state paradigm and provide two fully distributed key-generation paths.
The first is a black-box linear-algebraic assignment approach;
the second evaluates the trusted-dealer-style big-state key generation inside generic MPC using
an MPC-friendly PRG instantiation, trading custom distributed subprotocols for engineering
simplicity. The complete protocols and tradeoffs are deferred to the appendix. See \sectionref{sec:bigState} for details.
